# Research pipeline and stage gates

The process is **iterative, not a ritual that blocks ordinary exploration**. Evidence is kept separately from authority to advance. The live stage and next bounded action are in `research/STATE.json` and `research/NEXT_SESSION.md`.

| Stage | Work | Advance only when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| P0 — charter/bootstrap | Define scope, integrity rules, instruments and initial prior art | Repository checks and publication are verified |
| E1 — enumeration | Bound exact searches; record controls, independent test and negative cases | A precise observation plus immediate prior-art screen is filed, or choose next bounded exploration |
| E2 — pattern and hypothesis | Algebraic reduction, discriminating examples, conjecture and counterexample attack | Exact quantifiers, significance and plausible route are stated |
| N1 — deep novelty audit | Nearest theorems, standard identities, OEIS, books, papers, citation chains and recent preprints | Specific claimed increment survives as a defensible *candidate*; uncertainty is retained |
| M1 — mathematics | Proof or counterexample with independent checking | Every lemma/hypothesis is supported; false paths archived |
| N2 — post-result novelty audit | Repeat literature comparison against the actual theorem and proof, not the earlier conjecture | Distinct, worthwhile contribution remains plausible |
| F1 — optional formalisation | Lean 4 exact statement and proof, reproducible kernel build | Encoded theorem/proof matches the paper claim; no `sorry` or hidden axioms |
| V1 — optional registry | Palomar or other applicable independent mechanical checks | Actual external verification record obtained; never conflate with human review |
| W1 — write-up | Clear paper, computations, provenance and disclosure | Human author checks and approves mathematical content |
| A1 — preprint | Appropriate arXiv route | Separate explicit permission; preprint not peer review |
| J1 — journal | Venue AI policy, suitability and genuine refereeing | Separate explicit permission; journal submission/acceptance recorded accurately |

A negative novelty verdict **PARKS THAT CLAIM**, not the entire programme. Move to a materially different algebraic question; don't force a weak numerical curiosity into the publication pipeline. P0 ends without making a theorem or picking a paper title. E1 may involve genuine exploratory computation; N1 follows any worthwhile observation before a big proof campaign.

At each stage: maintain [claim distinctions](../research/CLAIMS.md), [candidate register](../research/CANDIDATES.md), [experiment register](../research/EXPERIMENT_LEDGER.md), [novelty ledger](../research/NOVELTY_LEDGER.md), [decisions](../research/DECISION_LOG.md), AI-use disclosure and append-only session record. When an existing theorem fully explains the observed pattern, record NOT_NOVEL and look for a new mechanism rather than relabelling the example.
