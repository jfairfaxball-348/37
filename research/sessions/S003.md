## Preregistered exact mathematical setup
Only p=31,37,41,43. Split p=31,37,43 are congruent to 1 mod 3; negative control 41 is congruent to 2 mod 3. For split primes least positive primitive roots g=3,2,3 respectively. Fix `chi(g^k)=omega^(k mod 3)`, `chi(0)=0`, `omega^2=-1-omega`. Coefficient representation `A+B omega` uses integer pairs with norm `A*A-A*B+B*B`, no floating approximations. Replacing chi by chi² conjugates `A+B omega -> (A-B)-B omega`. Inputs: **all** x in each F_p for Jacobi sums, class partitions, order-three cyclotomic counts and period convolutions; designated character probes x=2,3,10. No other prime/character/probe was added after results.

The Jacobi convention is `J(alpha,beta)=sum_{x in F_p} alpha(x)beta(1-x)` with nontrivial characters zero at 0. For nontrivial cubic chi, `J(chi,bar chi)=-chi(-1)=-1` because chi(-1)=1; for `J(chi,chi)`, norm p. Do not confuse `J(chi,chi)` with a differently conjugated pair, or B's orientation with the sign-free M in `4p=L²+27M²`. The classical primary coordinate normalization in these cases is reflected by B divisible by 3 and L=2A-B, M=B/3 for the fixed orientation; **this is not a novel identity**.

The exact independent oracle compares logarithm-derived character classes to cube-subgroup cosets, computes x+y=1 character-exponent counts independently of Jacobi pair summation, and obtains cubic period symmetric functions using additive convolution in Z[X]/(X^p−1), reducing rational expressions via 1+sum(zeta_p^i)=0. It checks the classical period polynomial `T^3+T^2-(p-1)/3*T-((L+3)p-1)/27` with **L≡1 mod 3**. No complex-root numerical interpolation or implicit choice of a 'primary prime' by sign.
