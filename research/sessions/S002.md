## Known theory before interpretation — not new mathematics
Let p be an odd prime, b≥2, p∤b, d=ord_p(b), s=v_p(b^d−1)≥1. LTE applied to b^d implies for every m≥1, v_p(b^(dm)−1)=s+v_p(m). Hence for all k≥1:
```
ord_(p^k)(b) = d * p^max(0,k-s).
```
For generalized repunit R_n(b)=(b^n−1)/(b−1):
- If b≡1 (mod p): v_p(R_n)=v_p(n), so the least length divisible by p^e is p^e, **not necessarily** ord_(p^e)(b).
- If b≢1 (mod p): v_p(R_n)=0 when d∤n; when d|n it equals s+v_p(n/d); least length p^e is d*p^max(0,e-s).
- If p|b: R_n(b)≡1 mod p for every n≥1; no finite positive repunit length is divisible by p.
- v_p(Phi_d(b))=s for d=ord_p(b) (including d=1, Phi_1(b)=b−1); higher cyclotomic factors contribute via the usual order/prime-power factorization. This is standard factorization plus LTE, not a new cyclotomic result.

For r in {1,...,p−1}, the polynomial f(X)=X^(p−1)−1 has f'(r)≢0 mod p. Hensel gives **exactly one** lift modulo p² for which b^d≡1 (mod p²), equivalently s≥2 or Fermat quotient q_p(b)=((b^(p−1)−1)/p)≡0 mod p. Under the shift r→r+pt, q_p(r+pt)≡q_p(r)−t*r^(−1) mod p, giving the unique t≡r*q_p(r) mod p. All these are **BACKGROUND / NOT_NOVEL**, irrespective of the computed p=37 examples.
