### NA-005 — exceptional lifts, orders and Fermat-quotient base shifts: NOT_NOVEL

In each nonzero residue r mod p exactly one lift b=r+tp modulo p² satisfies s>=2, or equivalently q_p(b)=0 (mod p). Here q_p(b)=(b^(p-1)-1)/p (mod p), and q_p(r+pt)=q_p(r)-t*r^(-1) (mod p), so t=r*q_p(r) (mod p). **Evidence:** complete p² unit class coverage p(p-1) and precisely p-1 exceptional classes for each control; changes under b→b+p reflect moving among these p lifts, not random trials.
